**Nation in northern Europa**

The United **Guilden** Provinces is a nation in northern Europa. It was once the center of a global trading empire, with control over key trading ports in {*South America*} and the Far East.

Guilden would remain neutral during the [[Great War]], providing refuge for those trying to escape the conflict. This policy of neutrality continues into the present, where it maintains close economic relations with both the [[Sterlian Commonwealth]] and neighboring [[Livron]] and [[Thalerland]], despite the tensions of the Cold War.
