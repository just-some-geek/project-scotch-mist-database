# Shenzhou People's Army (SPA)
The SPA is the official armed force of North Shenzhou
#### *Peasant, Proletariat, Patriot*
The SPA is an all-volunteer force, filled with the North's most loyal and motivated troops. Their discipline, courage, and revolutionary fervor enable them to fight exceptionally well in situations that would break most other armies.
#### *Spirit of the White Tiger*
The SPA's doctrine recommends that one fights "like a tiger", emphasizing careful observation and planning, stealth and infiltration tactics, and swift and overwhelming attacks. With this, the SPA is capable of decisively defeating numerically and materially superior forces with ease.
