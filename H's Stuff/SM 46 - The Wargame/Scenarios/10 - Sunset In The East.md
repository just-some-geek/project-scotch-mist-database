
**Combat Scenario X**
## Sunset In The East
The Fall of Kaigaishima
9 April 1958
Kaigaishima, Sterlian Far Eastern Territories

---
## Briefing:
So, it's come to this.

Chendai has fallen into Communist hands. Our armies, and those of our Southern allies, have shattered, and what remains are scattered to the winds. By our estimates, only 8% of our forces on the mainland got out, most without weapons or supplies. 

Negotiations with the Eikans have broken down. They say that this war does not end until all of Shenzhou is united under the Northern flag. In case you've forgotten, that includes Kaigaishima.

Parliament still thinks we stand a chance. They've sent a Royal Navy task force from the Europan fleet to shore up Kaigaishima's defenses. We expect them to arrive in 14 days, 10 if we're lucky.

Our orders are simple: Hold Kaigaishima.

This is our finest hour, Commander. Make the Commonwealth proud.

For King and Country.
