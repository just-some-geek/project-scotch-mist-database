The following is a list of endings for each main character story. Spoilers, obviously.
#Tier-2 #General 
# Olivia Northwind

## Sunset

Olivia's first ending where she decides to stay on the island.
## For King And Country (Secret Ending)

Olivia's second ending in which she leaves the island and returns back to the Sterlian capital.

# Basilio Liwanag

## Balikbayan 

Basilio's first ending, in where he flees and returns home to [[The Isabelines]] with the rest of the Kingfishers.
## Tadhana (Secret Ending)

Basilio's second ending, in where he continues his scholarly education in Avonforde University.
# Li Zenghui

## Mi Ultimo Adios

Joseph's basic ending, this ending is inevitable and there's not much you can do to change it. You are arrested and executed for allegedly orchestrating [[Red Christmas]] as well as leading a supposed uprising against the Sterlian Commonwealth.

## Thy Will Be Done - Sunrise (Secret Ending Route and Ending)

[[Thy Will Be Done]] is the secret ending route for Joseph in where you cheat death and live for just a bit longer. This will lead you to Joseph's secret ending titled Sunrise.


# Yukio Yamada

## Perpetual Motion

Yukio's first ending in where he flees with Basilio to his home country The Isabelines.
## First Law (Secret Ending)

Yukio's second ending in where he stays on the island with [[Rev. Thomas McKenzie]], finally finding the home.
