**Primary game mechanic**

The **Calendar System** is a dynamic system that handles the timing and order of events, questlines, and day-to-day activities during a playthrough of Scotch Mist.

# Overview
The **Calendar System** operates in a similar manner to ATLUS's Persona and Metaphor series: the game takes place over the course of 339 calendar days (from 1 June 1957 - 6 May 1958), with each day being divided into six distinct "time phases", for ~2034 phases in total.

>*Not all of these time phases are playable for all characters, since some phases have to be allocated for school, day jobs, or other mandatory activities. For the most part, however, these unplayable phases still contribute to the game's story.* 

Certain actions will advance time to the next phase, such as hanging out with a confidant, travelling between districts, and sleeping. Other activities may be locked behind the time of day, seasons, or time-bound events. Players must carefully manage and allocate these time phases and activities to make the most of their stay on Kaigaishima.
# Time Phases
Every day of the game is divided into six four-hour time phases, as such:

| Phase          | Midnight    | Early Morning | Morning     | Noon        | Afternoon   | Evening     |
| -------------- | ----------- | ------------- | ----------- | ----------- | ----------- | ----------- |
| **Time (24H)** | 2200 - 0159 | 0200 - 0559   | 0600 - 0959 | 1000 - 1359 | 1400 - 1759 | 1800 - 2159 |
# Time-Restricted Actions
Certain actions will only become available at certain times of the day
