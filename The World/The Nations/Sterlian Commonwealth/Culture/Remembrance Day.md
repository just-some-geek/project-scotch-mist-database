**Holiday commemorating the end of the Great War**

**Remembrance Day** is a memorial day observed in the [[Sterlian Commonwealth]] to honor members of the [[Sterlian Commonwealth Armed Forces]] who died in the line of duty. It has been observed every 11 November since the end of the [[Great War]] in 1928. The date commemorates the armistice signed by representatives of the Entente and [[Thalerland]] which ended hostilities on the Western Front "at the 11th hour, of the 11th day, of the 11th month" of 1928.
