**Alliance of Otesian nations**

The **Otesian Co-Prosperity Sphere**, or simply **The Sphere**, is a [[Otesia|pan-Otesian]] union established by the [[Eikan Empire]]. Initially composed of the Eikan Empire (including annexed {*Korea*}, [[North Shenzhou]], and {*Thailand*}, it later included [[Vostochnya]] and [[Tugrikia]], and may soon expand into the [[Far Eastern Territories]] and other nations in Southeast Otesia.

The Sphere's structure combines aspects of an economic bloc, a political union, and a military alliance.
