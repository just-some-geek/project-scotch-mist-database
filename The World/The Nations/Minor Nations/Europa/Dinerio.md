**Nation in southwest Europa**

The People's Republic of **Dinerio** is a nation in southwestern Europa and a former Great Power. Dinerio once ran the first Europan colonial empire, controlling territories from {*Mexico*} in the west to [[The Isabelines]] in the Far East. Despite the loss of its empire, it retained positive economic and diplomatic ties with its former colonies.

Once a monarchy, the country's transition to democracy was cut short by the outbreak of the [[Great War]] and the subsequent [[Reclamation Wars#Dineric Civil War|Dineric Civil War]].
