**Nation in central Europa**

**Schillerland**, also known as the **Twin Monarchy** and officially the **Twin Monarchy of the Schillerreich**, is a multi-national constitutional monarchy in central Europa. The empire is home to a diverse array of ethnic groups, each with their own language, culture, food, architecture, and so on. While great for tourism, this diversity causes immense sociopolitical tension and disunity.

Schillerland was a member of the **Pakt** during the [[Great War]], alongside [[Thalerland]], [[Liranium]], and [[Levgar]]. Following the 1926 October Revolt and subsequent withdrawal from the war, the Twin Monarchy would become largely ceremonial and its powers delegated to the Imperial Council. While not officially a part of [[The International]], Schillerland's close relations with its neighbors have ensured that the Syndicalists retain public sympathies, if not outright support.
