**Nation in East Otesia**

**Vostochnya**, officially the **Far Eastern Soviet Socialist Republic**, is a communist nation located in the northeast of [[Otesia]]. It borders the {Pacific} Ocean to the east, the Arctic Sea to the north, the {Siberian} Unconsolidated Territories to the west, and [[Tugrikia]], [[North Shenzhou]], and the [[Eikan Empire]] along its southern border.

The Republic formed during the collapse of the [[Kopeccya|Kopechian Empire]] during the October Revolution after a mutiny in the {Pacific} Fleet. With support from the Eikan Empire, the Red Army defended the six eastern oblasts until the end of the Kopechian Civil War in 1933. The nation saw significant economic development post-war, strengthening its ties with the Eikan Empire.

As of 1957, it is a full member of both the [[Otesian Co-Prosperity Sphere]] and [[The Internationale]].

The civil war left the Republic devastated.

# Territory
The Republic is composed of six oblasts, listed below from west to east.
## Yakutia
The farthest north of the oblasts, (Yakutia) is rich in the minerals, lumber, and petrochemicals that fuel the Republic's economy. However, its climate and terrain make it largely inhospitable, being covered in a near-permanent frost year-round.
## Buryatia & Chita
(Buryatchita) sits along the eastern shore of Lake (Baikal), forming Vostochnya's border with Tugrikia and North Shenzhou. 
## Amur
The (Amur) River, from which the oblast gets its name, is the main economic artery of the Republic.
## Khabarovsk
The largest and most populous of the oblasts, (Khabarovsk) is the Republic's industrial heartland.
## Magadan
The port of (Magadan) is the political center of the Republic, home to the naval base where the Revolution first took hold in the Far East.
## Kamchatka
The easternmost region of both Vostochnya and the Otesian continent. There is not much here in terms of both population and resources, save for the abundance of unremarkable coastline.
