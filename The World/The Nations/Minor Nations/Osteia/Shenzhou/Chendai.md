**Chendai** (kanji, lit. *translation*) is a major coastal city in [[South Shenzhou]]. It is the largest port city in [[Shenzhou]],

The city is the name-giver for the nearby [[Chendai Archipelago]], 

The city is also notable for its large population of foreigners, with the [[Sterlian Concession]] housing X00,000 foreign nationals. The Concession is the largest area on the mainland under direct control of the [[Sterlian Commonwealth]], and serves as a vital logistics hub supporting the war effort of South Shenzhou and their Commonwealth ally.
