**Student-activism organization**

**KUSU** is an joint student council and activist organization from [[St. Ignatius University]] and [[Paul Miki University]]. It is later known as the **Kaigaishiman Student Underground**. By 1957 it holds approximately 120 members with 60 members per university. 

#Tier-1 #Factions 

### Founding and General Operations
___ 

The organization was founded in 1926 by Benjamin Harper (SIU) and Josephine Tenenbaum (PMU) who became it's first head council members. It's vision and mission is to protect and promote the fundamental right to education and achieve genuine freedom and democracy through organized, nationwide student action. The actions include initiating discourse and discussion between students and public leaders, organizing rallies, and disseminating information. As of 1956 they began publication of their newsletter [[The Kingfisher]] which publishes every month. They operate two branches one in each university where they hold their university respective meetings in their auditoriums, they also occasionally host joint council meetings in [[Wildflour (bar)|the Wildflour]].

### Martial Law and the Kaigaishiman Underground
___

After the publication of the Northwind Papers as well as the declaration of Martial Law, tensions slowly increase amongst the students of KUSU. Members have been getting unjustly arrested and abducted for their association with KUSU and the few who are released or escape report about torture and human rights violations. A wave of infighting starts amongst the organization with one side calling for more disruptive and violent action while the other side remaining staunchly reformist. After some time, the majority ended up being the radicals causing a 40% member resignation from those who can't support the organization anymore. 

In the midst of this council member Lu Yuhan is contacted by Wildflour owner [[Damian Lim]] member of the Kaigaishiman Underground who convinces her to regroup the remaining members and reorganize the remaining members as the Kaigaishiman Student Underground (KSU), KU's new youth wing. Elisabeth Macleay rejects a leading position in the new KSU to focus on Kingfisher duties, shifting the publications focus to underground distribution and anti-propaganda pieces. The other council members step down as well leaving Lu Yuhan is left as the sole president of KSU as of the 27th of January, 1958.

Their duties include recruitment, mass protests and demonstrations against the current Sterlian regime, as well as collaboration and organization with the moderate opposition, worker unions and other members of the urban poor. Other KSU members join the armed wing of the KU, raiding military bases for arms and disrupting communications from KUs main operating from their base in the [[Hoshiyama Nature Reserve]]. Member count for the KSU is said to have increased to 2000 at it's peak, April 1st, 1958. 

On 6 February, in defiance of the ban against public demonstrations by governor [[Takatsugu Sasaki-Newman]], the students of PMU would hold a rally on-campus to protest the results of the [[Representation Referendum of 1958]]. The rally was coordinated through The Kingfisher. Government forces were quick to crack down on the demonstration, dispersing the students with tear gas and baton charges.

In late March 1958, pamphlets would begin circulating declaring that a public demonstration would be held on 9 April, "to bring an end to the senseless war in Shenzhou and the tyrannical government which demands that we fight it." The identity of the group publishing and circulating these pamphlets remains unknown, but some speculate that Kingfisher staff was involved. On the 9th of April the outside of PMU on McCormick Avenue is flooded with upwards of 10 thousand people with the rally starting at 9:30 AM in the morning. It lasted till 4:37 PM in the evening, ending due to gunshots being fired on the crowd.
