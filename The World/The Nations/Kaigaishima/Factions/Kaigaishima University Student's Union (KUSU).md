**Student-activism organization**

**KUSU** is an joint student council and activist organization from [[St. Ignatius University]] and [[Paul Miki University]]. It is later known as the **Kaigaishiman Student Underground**. By 1957 it holds approximately 120 members with 60 members per university. 

#Tier-1 #Factions 

### Founding and General Operations
___ 

The organization was founded in 1926 by Benjamin Harper (SIU) and Josephine Tenenbaum (PMU) who became it's first head council members. It's vision and mission is to protect and promote the fundamental right to education and achieve genuine freedom and democracy through organized, nationwide student action. The actions include initiating discourse and discussion between students and public leaders, organizing rallies, and disseminating information. As of 1956 they began publication of their newsletter [[The Kingfisher]] which publishes every month. They operate two branches one in each university where they hold their university respective meetings in their auditoriums, they also occasionally host joint council meetings in [[Wildflour (bar)|the Wildflour]].

### Martial Law and the Kaigaishiman Underground
___

After the publication of the Northwind Papers as well as the declaration of Martial Law, tensions slowly increase amongst the students of KUSU. Members have been getting unjustly arrested and abducted for their association with KUSU and the few who are released or escape report about torture and human rights violations. A wave of infighting starts amongst the organization with one side calling for more disruptive and violent action while the other side remaining staunchly reformist. After some time, the majority ended up being the radicals causing a 40% member resignation from those who can't support the organization anymore. In the midst of this council member Lu Yuhan is contacted by Wildflour owner [[Damian Lim]] member of the Kaigaishiman Underground who convinces her to reground and reorganize the remaining members as the Kaigaishiman Student Underground (KSU), KU's new youth wing. Elisabeth Macleay rejects a leading position in the new KSU to focus on Kingfisher duties, shifting the publications focus to underground distribution and anti-propaganda pieces. The other council members step down as well leaving Lu Yuhan is left as the sole president of KSU.
