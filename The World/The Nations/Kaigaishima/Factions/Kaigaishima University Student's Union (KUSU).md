**Student-activism organization**

**KUSU** is an joint student council and activist organization from [[St. Ignatius University]] and [[Paul Miki University]]. It is later known as the **Kaigaishiman Underground Students Union**

#Tier-1 #Factions 

### Founding and General Operations
___ 

The organization was founded in 1926 by Benjamin Harper (SIU) and Josephine Tenenbaum (PMU) who became it's first head council members. It's vision and mission is to protect and promote the fundamental right to education and achieve genuine freedom and democracy through organized, nationwide student action. The actions include initiating discourse and discussion between students and public leaders, organizing rallies, and disseminating information. As of 1956 they began publication of their newsletter [[The Kingfisher]] which publishes every month. They operate two branches one in each university and occasionally host joint meetings in [[Wildflour (bar)|the Wildflour]]. 

### The Kaigaishiman Crisis
___

In the year of 1957, KUSU's activities and engagements mainly revolve around protesting against the current [[Shenzhou War|war in Shenzhou]]. On the 21st of September it is announced that [[Representation Referendum of 1958]] will be held on the 9th of January to break the political deadlock in [[Sterlian Parliament|Parliament]] over a bill granting more political representation to the colonies. Council member Lu Yuhan brings forth the issue on whether or not KUSU should take a stance for or against independence. The decision reached is that KUSU's organizational stance should remain neutral, with it's intention to be educating fellow students and the masses about what the referendum means. It is used as an opportunity to utilize The Kingfisher more as it has scaled up in production and reach. 

On the 25th of October, a [[Olivia Northwind|whistle blower]] contacts KUSU via members Basilio Liwanag and Yukio Yamada and leaks information regarding the Commonwealth's corruption and war crimes in Shenzhou. This includes budget cuts to public works and humanitarian aid, the use of chemical weapons, and intentional targeting of civilian centres. In addition it includes a plan to rig the results of the referendum. This information is kept secret between the council members and The Kingfisher, whose focus shifts to proving the validity of these claims independently of the whistle blower. Notably, member Yukio sneaking into [[City Hall]] and [[Albertine Valley Country Club]] to extract valuable information. 

The information is shown to be valid and The Kingfisher starts establishing contacts with an [[Eleanor Carter|IBS insider]] as well as multiple local business and shop keeps in preparation for the expose titled the [[Northwind Papers]]. The papers are successfully released on the 19th of December, public reaction is justifiably intense. However on Christmas day four coordinated bombings across the island of Kaigaishima, perpetrated by an "unknown terrorist organization". Four locations were hit, all of which were government-operated or affiliated, known as [[Red Christmas]].

Following these attacks [[Martial Law in Kaigaishima|martial law]] is declared on Kaigaishima subsequently postponing the referendum vote indefinitely
