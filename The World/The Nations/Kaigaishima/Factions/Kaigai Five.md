**Suspected orchestrators of the Red Christmas attacks**

The **Kaigai Five** was a cabal in [[Kaigaishima]] which are believed to have orchestrated the [[Red Christmas]] terrorist attacks. The group was composed of high-ranking members of the [[Sterlian Commonwealth Armed Forces|Sterlian military]] and government, executives of major corporations, and leaders of major crime syndicates on the island. While known to the public as the Five, the conspiracy likely reached much deeper into high society and may have involved up to 100 members.

An unreleased version of the [[Northwind Papers]] would have exposed the conspiracy, with five people being definitively identified as leaders of the plot: [[Robert Gilmoure]], [[Andrea Byrd]], [[Stephen Edwards]], [[Adrian Wagner]], and [[Chen Wing-Hung]]. However, the version of the exposé released to the general public through the [[Imperial Broadcasting Service]] would have this section removed for unknown reasons. As such, none of the Kaigai Five would be prosecuted for any crimes.

## Background
In the aftermath of the [[Parliamentary Crisis of 1953]], the Commonwealth's government was strongly divided between the reformist [[Labour Party]] and the conservative [[Nationalist Party]]. The Special Election of 1953 resulted in a Nationalist cabinet taking power in the federal government, but many colonial governments found themselves under Labour, Kaigaishima included.
