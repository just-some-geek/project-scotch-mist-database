**Bonapartic-era bastion fortress**

**Fort Valkyrie** is a fortress and historical landmark in [[Districts of Kaigaishima#**Yilan**|Yilan, Kaigaishima]]. Situated atop **Valkyrie Peak**, the fortress once served as the main base for the [[Sterlian Commonwealth Armed Forces|Sterlian garrison]] on [[Kaigaishima]]. After the end of the [[Great War]], control of the fort was transferred to the [[Imperial Broadcasting Service]] Eastern Division, which is now headquartered within.

# Architecture
The fort is constructed in a hybrid style, mixing elements of Europan-style star or bastion fortresses and traditional Eikan castles, owing to the multi-phase construction and unique geography of Valkyrie Peak.

The overall layout of the fort resembles a Sengoku-era Eikan hill castle, with a series of courtyards and gatehouses radiating out from a central tower keep. 
# History
## Initial Construction
The earliest construction of note atop Valkyrie Peak was a set of stone-encased signal fires dating back to 0000. These were once part of a network of beacons along the eastern coast of Kaigaishima used by navigators making their way into the [[Port of Kaigaishima|harbor]] in the west. These signal fires were disassembled and transferred to the [[Sterlian Museum of the East]], while a replica stands in its place within Fort Valkyrie's courtyard.

The first fortifications were constructed around 0000 by the {*Eikans or Shenzhen?*}. The fort comprised a small hilltop keep, a square courtyard, and the surrounding stone wall, as was standard practice for the time. This first fort would be demolished by a fire in 0000, and the site would only be partially reconstructed by the time the Sterlian Empire would arrive in force.

Upon the acquisition of Kaigaishima by the Sterlian Empire in 0000, the fortifications atop Valkyrie Peak were expanded. The inner keep would be retrofitted with modern gun emplacements and signals technology, and outer bastions would be constructed or carved out of the slopes. These outer fortifications would eventually turn into a sprawling underground network of tunnels, shafts, and bunkers by the time the Great War broke out in 1914.
## Great War
Though Sterlia, Eiko, and Shenzhou were all involved in the fighting in the Far East, Kaigaishima never came under direct attack during the Great War. As such, Fort Valkyrie was primarily used as a supply depot and training facility for troops being transported through the island going to the Far Eastern front.

During the middle years of the war, the upper and port-facing sections of the fort would be refurbished with larger-caliber guns and anti-aircraft emplacements. Most notable among these was the casemate installation at **Battery 1**, which housed dual-mounted 20" naval cannons originally built for the scrapped super-dreadnought HMS Valkyrie.

The proliferation of civilian radios during the war prompted the [[Ministry of Public Enlightenment and Propaganda|Ministry of Public Affairs]] to construct a broadcast tower within the fort to facilitate radio and television broadcasts.
## Conversion to IBS Facility
Following the end of the Great War in 1928, the Kaigaishiman colonial government acquired the nearby island of Higashijima (lit. *East Island*) from Shenzhou. The island, now Kaigaishima's [[Districts of Kaigaishima#East Region|East Region]], was converted into a major military installation, leaving facilities on the island proper open for new residents.

The size, location, and existing infrastructure made the fort an optimal location for the recently-nationalized IBS East to expand their operations.
