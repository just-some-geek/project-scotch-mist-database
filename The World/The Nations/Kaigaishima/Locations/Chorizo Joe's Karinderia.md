**Restaurant chain in Kaigashima**

**Chorizo Joe's Karinderia** (Sterlian: Chorizo Joe's Eatery) is a [[Kaigaishima|Kaigaishiman]] restaurant chain with 6 locations around the island. The menu consists mainly of traditional [[The Isabelines|Sabelinyo]] food made in the home-kitchen style.

# Menu
The eatery serves a wide range of traditional Sabelinyo dishes, most of which are prepared in the home-cooked style. The menu varies slightly between locations and times of day and year, owing to the availability of supplies and specialties of the staff.

The chain is most famous for its chorizo (also known as *longganisa*), which is a sausage made primarily from ground pork, though chicken, beef, or even tuna are sometimes used. These are spiced with garlic, pepper, salt, muscovado, and other spices. Two varieties are served: the sweet *jamonado* and the garlicky *derecado*, both in *ala carte* links and as part of rice meals.
