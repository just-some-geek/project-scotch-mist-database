**Military airport in Kaigaishima

**Horikoshi International Airport** (KGS, HRKS) is a joint-use airport on [[Kaigaishima]] operated by the [[Sterlian Commonwealth Armed Forces]]. It is often referred to as **Kaigaishima Int'l Airport**, **Kaigaishima Airport**, and **RAF Horikoshi** when discussing its military use. The facility is built on reclaimed and levelled land in the Yamane Bay.

The airport is named for [[Eikan Empire|Eikan]] aeronautics engineer **Rei Horikoshi**, designer of the J4-Z fighter plane.
