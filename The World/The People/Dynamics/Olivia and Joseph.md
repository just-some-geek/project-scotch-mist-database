**The relationship between Olivia Northwind and Li Zenghui**

This page describes the dynamic and relationship between the characters [[Olivia Northwind]] and [[Li Zenghui]]. 

#General  #Tier-2

Joseph and Olivia's relationship could often be mistaken for that of an on and off, will they won't they couple. However it could not be that in the slightest as it was never on in the first place. Initially it was born out of dependence, almost parasitical in nature. Both parties approached each other not with the intention of forming something of substance or meaning, but with the intention of using the other for personal advancement and gain. Yet the more they use each other, the more they unwillingly learn more of each others circumstance and being, gaining a deeper understanding with each passing day.

To the point where by the time Joseph is accused or orchestrating [[Red Christmas]], they realize their dependence on each other, not just politically but emotionally as well. They will never find romance as Joseph is fated to die in almost every outcome, but in the extremely rare chance that he does survive, there will always be an evergreen boundary that will prevent them from seeing other as more. The bounder being that they see one another and recognize each other's potential but not clear enough to pursue anything further due to physical and moral obligations. More than friends, never lovers.

**Joseph**

Initially, Joseph regards Olivia on a surface level as a useful asset into establishing his high school down south, valuing her involvement mainly for securing building permits, obtaining materials, and anything pertaining to the quality control of its construction.

Later on, he observes that as she begins to care more deeply and involve herself more in its fruition, Joseph comes to believe wholeheartedly in the genuine change she is capable of initiating, not merely by virtue of the position and authority she holds.

**Olivia**

When Joseph first proposed his plans to establish a high school in Yilan, Olivia met the idea with measured skepticism. As authoritative figure, she has experienced idealistic projects collapse under the weight of poor planning or worse, hidden agendas. However, Joseph seems to be an exception as he did not present himself as a person seeking political leverage. Intrigued, Olivia eventually recognized his intentions and belief that education could change the nation's future, and agreed to lend her support.

What struck Olivia most was Joseph's impartiality. He never held her at a position where she had to prove her worth beyond the position she already has. This quiet neutrality was unexpectedly disarming, because for once, she did not feel scrutinized or tested, and the constant pressure to justify her competence seemed to ease in his presence.

Obviously, this realization did not come to her immediately, and only emerged over time, maybe through shared nights and drinks. She noticed that Joseph was consistent in his actions and didn't speak merely to fill silence, which Olivia found herself believing in his resolve more. It was this reliability, more than his ideals alone, that earned her respect in which she never felt the pressure of calculated rhetoric and political posturing.
