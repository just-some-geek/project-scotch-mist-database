### Dev Notes
---

Haliya was born into a family of engineers, where discipline and practicality shaped everyday life. Her parents believed deeply in the value of structure out of a quiet conviction that stability was a form of care. They wanted her to be capable and prepared for a world they understood to be unforgiving.

From an early age, however, Haliya found herself drawn to art. She spent time sketching in the margins of her notebooks, interested in how emotion could be expressed without strict rules. Despite this inclination, her parents mainly recognized her strong aptitude for technical subjects and encouraged her to develop it, sometimes firmly. Expectations in their household were high, and mistakes were often met with insistence. On multiple occasions, she had to rework solutions not as punishment but as a belief she could do better. Eventually, the repetition often felt suffocating.

Ironically, though, she excelled. Logic came naturally to her, and she developed a strong ability to recognize patterns and solve problems with careful attention. There were even moments when she questioned whether she would have pursued these skills on her own, even without her family’s influence.

As she grew older, Haliya chose to focus more on practical fields, particularly computers, believing they would allow her to make a meaningful impact. Initially, this shift felt like a compromise and a concession. She was uneasy with how quickly she adapted, as if enjoying it meant leaving behind a more authentic part of herself.

Over time, however, her perspective changed. She began to see similarities between computing and the creative disciplines she valued. This realization led her to explore ways to bridge her passions, integrating patterns from music and other creative metrics into her work. Her early work was experimental and did not always align with established approaches, but it showed a distinct and original way of thinking.

No one believed her at first, but with the eventual developments of computer programming, she eventually attracted attention beyond her immediate environment. A group of researchers from Kaigaishima took interest in her work and offered her a working scholarship at Paul Miki University. There, she saw an opportunity to pursue both her technical abilities and creative interests without having to choose between them.


### Personality
___

Haliya is, by her own admission, a messy perfectionist. She cares deeply about the quality of her work and often holds herself to high standards, but she struggles with consistency when it comes to planning and organization. She tends to move in bursts. Fully absorbed when something captures her attention, then scattered when it comes to maintaining structure. Because of this, she sometimes doubts whether her working scholarship will push her to improve, especially given the leeway it allows her to escape classes. Still, she remains committed to her work, even if her process doesn’t always follow a clear or orderly path.

Despite her technical focus, her artistic habits persist in quieter ways. She often doodles absentmindedly in her notebook, filling the margins with sketches and patterns. If noticed, however, she is quick to brush it off or deny it entirely, treating it as something incidental rather than something she actively values.
