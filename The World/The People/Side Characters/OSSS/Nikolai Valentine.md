**Nikolai Valentine** is an [[Office of Special and Strategic Services (Columbia)|OSSS]] agent deployed to Kaigaishima alongside his partner, [[Isaiah Harland]].

#Tier-4 #Characters 

### Dev Notes
---

Though the OSSS has far more competent (or at least more focused) agents in the region, by some twist of fate Mr. Valentine was the one sent in to “cover up” Harland’s “screwups”. In practice, his hilarious hijinks are likely doing more to hurt Columbian interests than advance them.

### Personality / Tidbits
___

-  Wears an eye mask with blue ‘eyelids’ on it.
-  Only has one glove on his left hand – never seen with a right glove.
-  Almost never seen without his black fedora. 
-  He is a Columbian citizen but is [[Livron|half-Livronnaise]], and claims to be the rightful heir to the Livronnaise throne despite Livron not having a monarchy.
-  Claims that he is the descendant of the last Livronnaise king.
-  May or may not have tuberculosis.
