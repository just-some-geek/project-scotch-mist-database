**A letter addressed to Yukio Yamada from Makoto Satoh Carter

**Departure** is a letter written by [[Makoto Carter|Makoto Satoh]] early 1957, addressed to now [[Yukio Yamada]]. Detailing their motive and reasoning for fleeing the Eikan Empire as an explanation for Yukio.
Written in Sterlian possibly as a way to evade getting caught.

#Tier-2 #Media

	Dear Yukio, 

	I know you must be wondering about my whereabouts, and concerned about my safety. 
	First of all I want to assure you that I am safe and well. 
	However, I am writing to tell you this, and I know this will be difficult to hear.
	
	As of writing, I am no longer in Eiko. I have managed to sneak on a flight out of here,
	since the empire opened up it's borders and I currently en-route to an island in the south.
	This may be news for you to hear, and please do not take this action of mine as a result of you.
	Please dont. I am doing this because I am also terrified of the direction this place is taking.
	Everyone has become more and more angry towards any outsider, and the culture's emphasis on order
	and control has made living here suffocating. Now people our age are expected to enlist in the military?
	I refuse to live in a place like that, I fear for my life. 
	
	I am currently making my way to the Sterlian colony of Kaigaishima, from there I can hopefully enroll 
	into a university, get a stable job and find myself a better home and future. 
	You know about the stories we've heard about that place? The culture there feels like it would be 
	more accepting to people like us, since it's filled with a bunch of different people.
	While I'm not certain about that, it's a risk I'm willing to take. 
	
	I hope you understand my decision, I hope you don't feel betrayed by me choosing to leave, but I
	must look after myself first and foremost. I know we've talked about leaving before, and I know you
	would've wished to come with me, but as the plan was currently set up, it would've been too dangerous
	for you to have been aware. However, if you wish to follow, I have attached to this letter instructions.
	Instructions on how to smuggle your way out of this island, as well as a way to forge documents. 
	
	I know I can't be there with you right now, and I can't control what decision you make.
	But I trust that you'll make the right decision.
	
	Stat safe Yukio, please.
	
	Makoto
