**A letter of acceptance to Saint Ignatius University


#Tier-3 #Media

	Dear Mr. Liwanag,
	
	We are pleased to inform you that your application for scholarship to Saint Ignatius University has been accepted! 
	It is with great enthusiasm that we write to congratulate you on your admission to the Class of 53. 
	Our community has never been more in need of intelligent, strong and committed visionary young people. 
	I personally am delighted that we have chosen you to be among those at SIU, I hope you will join our community this autumn.
	
	For over 50 years, our motto of ‘Lux in Domino’ - “Light in The Lord” has guided our students, faculty and alumni in their pursuit of integrity, 
	excellence and service. As a student of SIU, you will engage in astonishing intellectual, cultural and scientific resources. 
	Your education will equip you with the knowledge and wisdom to make an impact in any field, anywhere in the world.  
	
	I recognize that this news is accompanied by the challenge of choosing your new home without the benefit of visiting us this spring, 
	but we assure you that you will be given a dormitory to live in, most expenses paid. 
	
	With my congratulations and best wishes,
	
	Benjamin Harper,
	Director of International Recruitment
	
	Saint Ignatius University
