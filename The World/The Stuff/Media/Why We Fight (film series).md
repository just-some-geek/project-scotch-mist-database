**Sterlian propaganda film series**

**Why We Fight** is a series of propaganda films produced by the [[Ministry of Public Affairs|Sterlian Ministry of Public Affairs]] between 1915 to 1955. These focused on informing the [[Sterlian Commonwealth|Sterlian]] public on the nations that the [[Sterlian Commonwealth Armed Forces]] were deployed to during the [[Great War]] and [[Reclamation Wars]].
