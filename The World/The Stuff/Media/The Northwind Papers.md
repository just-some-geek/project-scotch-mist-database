The **Northwind Papers** were a series of documents concerning the crimes of the [[Sterlian Commonwealth|Sterlian government]] in the Far East, most notably government corruption, electoral fraud, and the use of chemical and biological weapons by the [[Sterlian Commonwealth Armed Forces|SCAF]] in [[Shenzhou War|Shenzhou]].
