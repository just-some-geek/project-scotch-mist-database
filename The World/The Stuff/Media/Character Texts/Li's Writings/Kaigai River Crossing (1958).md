**Novel written by Li Zenghui**

**Kaigai River Crossing** is a novel written by [[Li Zenghui]] during his decade long leave from Kaigaishima, a sequel to his previous novel [[Chasing Starry Mountain (1954)]]. The title in reference to the two bodies of water that separates the North, Central and South regions, as "crossing the river" is a phrase used to describe people who move up in social class.
