The **Northwind Papers** were a series of documents concerning the crimes of the [[Sterlian Commonwealth|Sterlian government]] in the Far East, most notably government corruption, electoral fraud, and the use of chemical and biological weapons by the [[Sterlian Commonwealth Armed Forces|SCAF]] in [[Shenzhou War|Shenzhou]].

The papers were first released in late December of 1957 through a [[The Kingfisher|local school newspaper]], though larger media outlets would quickly pick up the story in the following days. Public discourse around the exposé stopped abruptly in the wake of the [[Red Christmas]] attacks, as media outlets were shut down or put under tighter government censorship. Still, the papers caused irreparable reputational damage to the floundering Sterlian government in Kaigaishima, and is cited as a major contributing factor to the [[Kaigaishiman Crisis]].

Though many attribute the name to the whistleblower, [[Olivia Northwind]], contemporary sources actually named the exposé for her father, [[Nelson Charles Northwind|Admiral Northwind]], the main person implicated.
