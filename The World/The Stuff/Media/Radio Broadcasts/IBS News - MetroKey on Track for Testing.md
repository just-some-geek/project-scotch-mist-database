**Radio broadcast**

*This is Eleanor Carter for Imperial Broadcasting Service East.*

*The Ministry of Transportation announced today that the MetroKey development program is set to begin field testing in Kaigaishima at the end of this month.*

*The MetroKey is, as one engineer puts it, a "automatic train ticket" intended to replace the paper passbooks currently in use in the MetroPass program. They claim that the new device will help reduce operational costs and paper waste, as well as save commuters time spent fiddling around with ride fares.

*Citizens wishing to participate should inquire at the nearest MoTr* (motor) *Office for instructions.*

*We should all look forward to a world where the daily commute is a little less stressful.*
