**Radio broadcast**

*This is Eleanor Carter for Imperial Broadcasting Service East.*

*The eyes of the horse racing world turn to Takanabe Racecourse as the Orient Stakes, third and final leg of the Triple Crown, takes place at the Albertine Valley Country Club this Sunday.*

*The star of this year's show is three-year-old Pretty Derby, double-crown winner of the Kaigaishiman 2000 Guineas and the Kaigai Derby. Should they win the Orient Stakes, they'll be the first horse to win the Kaigaishiman Triple Crown undefeated.*

*However, experts say that Pretty Derby can expect a tough run from the competition, namely rivals Kingsman and Yonder Rhapsody. Both competitors have shown a preference for power and benefit greatly from the longer distance of the Orient Stakes.*

*The race scheduled for the afternoon of Sunday, October 28, as the 11th race on the 12-race card. Tickets are in short supply, but the IBS will broadcast the race live on the day itself. Betting, meanwhile, remains available both at the racecourse and dedicated offsite stations throughout the city.*
