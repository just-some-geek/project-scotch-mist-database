## Phase 1
In the early hours of the implementation of Martial Law on Kaigaishima, 

- Early response to the Red Christmas attacks are haphazard
- Military forces present:
	- Kaigaishima's garrison (well-trained, but not that many)
	- Local militia/neighborhood watch (poorly-trained, confined to their home districts)
- General paranoia and lack of training lead to an ineffective implementation of security measures
	- i.e. players are still able to move around mostly unhampered
	- arrests are happening, but are sporadic and inaccurate

## Phase 2
- The SCAF are brought in to properly secure Kaigaishima
	- some are rotated out of Shenzhou
	- some are fresh troops from the other Territories
