**Annual festival in Kaigaishima**

The **Summer Festival**, sometimes called the **Star Festival** (星祭り, _Hoshimatsuri_), is a festival held annually in [[Kaigaishima]] to commemorate {*something*} (represented by the stars Vega and Altair). It originates from the Eikan [*Tanabata festival*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tanabata), though it is observed earlier in Kaigaishima as compared to the Eikan mainland. It is typically held in the first week of July, culminating in the main festival on 7 July.
