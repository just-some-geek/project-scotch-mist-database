**Sterlian chemical warfare campaign in the Shenzhou War**

**Operation Ploughshare** was the codename given to the [[Sterlian Commonwealth|Sterlian]] chemical warfare program during the [[Shenzhou War]]. The operation targeted the rural areas of [[South Shenzhou]] using [[Agent Scarlet]] to destroy food stockpiles and guerilla bases. Due to the restrictions on chemical weapons post-[[Great War]], the chemical defoliant was smuggled into Shenzhou and deployed via air cargo.

The operation ran for several years at varied intensity until December 1957, when it was exposed in the [[Northwind Papers]]. The discovery and exposure of Ploughshare is cited as one of the main catalysts for the [[Kaigaishiman Crisis]].

Approximately 76 million liters of Agent Scarlet was deployed across the South, causing lasting ecological damage, socioeconomic disturbance, and health issues in the affected areas.
