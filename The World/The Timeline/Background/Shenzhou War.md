**War in Shenzhou from 1926-1959**

The **Shenzhou War**, also known as the **Far Eastern Emergency**, was an armed conflict fought on the [[Shenzhou]] mainland and the Sterlian-controlled [[Chendai Archipelago]] between [[South Shenzhou]] (Federation of Shenzhou) and [[North Shenzhou]] (People's Republic of Shenzhou) and their allies.

The fighting spilled into the [[Kaigaishiman Crisis]] in 1957, which further escalated into the **Great Eastern War** in 1958.

# Background
## End of the Great War
>*Main Article: [[Great War#Shenzhou]]*

In 1917, the Kingdom of [[Shenzhou]] would side with the {*Central Powers*} in the [[Great War]] due to longstanding grievances against the [[Eikan Empire|Eikan]] and [[Sterlian Commonwealth|Sterlian Empires]]. The Shenzhen would see great successes early in the war, largely driving the Entente out of their holdings in the south. However, a large Sterlian force in the south (composed primarily of troops from [[Newstrynia]] and the [[Sterlian Raj]]) would hold out in the city of [[Chendai]]. This force, alongside an Eikan army invading from the north, would slowly dismantle the Shenzhen state and force its surrender by 1921.

In the subsequent **Treaty of Chendai**, the monarchy would be deposed and replaced with a democratic government. Additionally, the nation would be divided between the victors: the remainder of the [[Chendai Archipelago]] and the city of Chendai would be annexed by the Sterlians, while the Eikans would annex some border regions in {*Manchuria*}. The remainder of the country would be split along the 34th parallel, with a Sterlian occupation zone in the south and an Eikan zone in the north.
