# Background
## End of the Great War
>*Main Article: [[Great War#Shenzhou]]*

In 1917, the Kingdom of [[Shenzhou]] would side with the {*Central Powers*} in the [[Great War]] due to longstanding grievances against the [[Eikan Empire|Eikan]] and [[Sterlian Commonwealth|Sterlian Empires]]. The Shenzhen would see great successes early in the war, largely driving the Entente out of their holdings in the south. However, a large Sterlian force in the south (composed primarily of troops from [[Newstrynia]] and the [[Sterlian Raj]]) would hold out in the city of [[Chendai]]. This force, alongside an Eikan army invading from the north, would slowly dismantle the Shenzhen state and force its surrender by 1921.

In the subsequent **Treaty of Chendai**, the monarchy would be deposed and replaced with a democratic government: the Republic of Shenzhou. Additionally, the nation would be divided between the victors: the city of Chendai and the remainder of the [[Chendai Archipelago]] would be annexed by the Sterlians, while the Eikans would annex some border regions in {*Manchuria*}. The remainder of the country would be split along the 34th parallel, with a Sterlian occupation zone in the south and an Eikan zone in the north.
## Partition of Shenzhou
The occupation zones in Shenzhou was intended to be a temporary measure while the democratic government got off the ground. In practice, however, the split administration would cause an irreconcilable rift within the fledgling Republic. More communist-leaning officials would take power in the North, while a more totalitarian party found themselves in power in the South.
