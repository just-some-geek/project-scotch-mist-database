Shenzhou (神州, pinyin: *shen2 zhou1*; Wade-Giles: *Shenchou*) - Divine province
	- Also the name of a Chinese spacecraft (神舟, the Divine Vessel)
	- and a Chinese city (深州, the Deep State?!)

Lantai (蓝泰, pinyin: *lan2 tai4*; Wade-Giles: *Lan-t'ai*) - Great Blue
	- The *tai* is the same as in Taishan, one of the five Sacred Mountains, apparently.

Qingtian (清天, pinyin: *qing1 tian1*; Wade-Giles: *Ch'ing-T'ian*) - Clear Sky
	- Same *qing* as the Qing Dynasty (清朝, Qing1 chao2)
	- Qingtian (青田) is also a county in Zhejiang, notable for being the hometown of many overseas Chinese, esp. in Europe

Diaogou dao (钓钩岛, pinyin: *diao4 gou1 dao3*; Wade-Giles: *Tiao-kou-tao*) - Fishhook Island
	- That weird fishhook island in the middle of the Inland Sea of Shenzhou

Neizhong hai (内场格) - inner central sea
	- Formal name for the Inland Sea, which connects the major cities of South Shenzhou
