To strip the story of the plot, characters and world and just talk about what it means to me. It's a story about a set of characters in a time of great political change and turmoil in a very diverse connected city, using their unique background, skills, education and privilege to operate in society as well as progress it. In the end the characters will face tragedy and destruction, but will ultimately push through because the human spirit is an unstoppable force. That sounds incredibly vague but trust me when I explain the entire thing it'll make sense.

A couple of reasons why I wanted to write a story like this can be listed into some points :

- I was really inspired by my trips to Hong Kong and Singapore, seeing people from different cultures and backgrounds working together
- I was inspired by Orb On the Movements of the Earth, an anime where characters across decades push for scientific progress but aren't remembered
- I was inspired by Fear and Hunger Termina : a game where characters use the human spirit to defeat eldtrich evils
- I was inspired by Garnt Maneetahpos Baan and just the ability to turn your creative vision into a finalized product
- I wanted to write something that gave me a bit of hope, in contrast to the despair you feel about the world nowadays
- I wanted to include a bunch of references to Filipino history (Martial Law, Jose Rizal, etc.) but not have it set in the Philippines
- I just think it's fun and fulfilling to do
