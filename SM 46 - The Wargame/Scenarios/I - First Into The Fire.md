
**Combat Scenario I**
## First Into The Fire  
The Invasion of South Shenzhou
20 June 1946
{*Tianjin*} Province, South Shenzhou

---
## Briefing:
Good evening, Commander. We’ve got a crisis on our hands.

30 minutes ago, elements of the Shenzhen People’s Army crossed the border into South Shenzhou. They’ve broken through the defences at the border, and will soon overrun the Republic’s army if we don’t step in.

You’ve been given command of the Sterlian Expeditionary Force, or at least the parts of it that are in-country. Reservists across the Far East and the Dominions are being mobilized, but they’ll have to wait for Parliament’s approval before they can be sent in. The forces you have are all you’re going to get, at least for the time being.

You are authorized to use any means necessary to halt the SPA’s advance. However, the Admiralty will not allow any Sterlian units to strike north of the border. Something about the Eikan Empire threatening a counter-intervention.

Godspeed, Commander. Make the Commonwealth proud.

---
## Objectives:
- Hold [border city] for at least 7 days.
- Ensure at least 30% of the South Shenzhen army survives.
- Do not give the Eikan Empire a reason to intervene.
